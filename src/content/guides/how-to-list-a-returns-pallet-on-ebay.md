## 1. Sort and test before you list

A returns pallet is a mix: sealed stock, opened boxes, used items and faulty ones, often with parts missing. Listing before you sort is how wrong conditions and returns happen.

- Unpack in batches and give each item a spot on a shelf or table.
- Power up and test anything electrical. Check that every part, cable and manual is there.
- Write the condition and any fault on a sticky note on the item, so it travels with it.
- Put items with no barcode in their own pile. They need a listing written from scratch.

## 2. Give every item its eBay condition

eBay's condition names change by category. For electronics, the ones a pallet needs are:

| What you have | Condition on eBay.com | On eBay.co.uk | ID |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sealed, unused, undamaged, in its box | New | New | `1000` |
| Opened, never used, no wear | Open box | Opened – never used | `1500` |
| Used, works as it should | Used | Used | `3000` |
| Faulty or not fully working | For parts or not working | For parts or not working | `7000` |

If you are unsure, pick the lower condition and say exactly what you found in the description: "box opened, all parts present", "small scratch on the lid", "charger missing". A buyer who gets what they read doesn't send it back.

## 3. Check what each one sells for

In Seller Hub, open the **Research** tab. Product research shows up to three years of eBay sales; search by the product name, or filter by its UPC or EAN.

Compare like with like: a sealed item and an opened one sell for different prices. Set your price from sales in the same condition, not from what is listed.

## 4. Photograph everything that isn't new

eBay does not allow stock photos for used, damaged or defective items. Photograph the actual item, including any marks or faults, with at least one photo of 500 pixels or more on the longest side. Plain photos only: eBay does not allow borders, added text or watermarks.

## 5. List the lot in bulk

Group the items by condition, put each group's barcodes in a spreadsheet, and upload it as drafts: see [how to bulk list on eBay from a spreadsheet](/guides/how-to-bulk-list-on-ebay-from-a-spreadsheet). Then add your photos and publish: see [how to publish your eBay drafts in bulk](/guides/how-to-publish-ebay-drafts-in-bulk).

## Where your drafts are

Every draft is in two places: the **Manage drafts** page in Seller Hub, and **Drafts** in My eBay. Drafts you uploaded from a spreadsheet land there too.

eBay keeps up to 5,000 drafts made in Seller Hub, and up to 250 made with the quick listing tool or the eBay app.

## 1. Choose the drafts to finish

On Manage drafts, tick the box beside each draft you want, or tick **Select all**. Group drafts that need the same things, such as one category or one postage service, so you fill those in once.

## 2. Open them together

Choose **Resume drafts**. Your drafts open together in eBay's bulk listing tool, which handles up to 2,000 at a time.

## 3. Add what each draft is missing

Drafts from a spreadsheet are usually missing the same four things:

| Missing | What to add |
|---|---|
| Photos | At least one per listing, 500 pixels or more on the longest side. eBay recommends more than one. |
| Item specifics | Brand, model, colour and the rest for the category. Required ones are flagged. |
| Postage | The service and cost, or your postage policy. |
| Returns | Your returns terms, or your returns policy. |

If you sell a lot, set up postage and returns once as business policies and pick them for every draft.

## 4. Check, then list

Fix anything the tool flags, check the prices, then list the drafts. Anything you leave stays a draft for later.

## How long drafts last

Drafts expire **75 days after they were last edited**. Editing a draft starts the 75 days again, but don't leave a batch for weeks: prices and stock move.

## The slow part, and how to skip it

The item specifics take the time: every draft, every field, often from the product page.

[Remvo](/) fills them in from the barcode, along with the title, category, parcel and a price from live eBay listings. With bulk listing you get the eBay drafts file to upload and a full CSV with every item specific beside it, so you copy rather than look things up. A listing costs $0.30 (£0.24), and your first one is free.

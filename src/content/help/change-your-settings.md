## 1. Choose your eBay site

In **Profile**, under **Selling**, choose **eBay site** and pick one of seven: eBay.com, eBay.ca, eBay.co.uk, eBay.de, eBay.fr, eBay.it or eBay.es. New listings are written for that site, in its language, with its prices. Listings you have already made stay as they are.

## 2. Choose your currency

**Currency** sets what top-ups are priced in: US dollars, euros or pounds. Your credits are the same in any currency.

## 3. Private or business seller

**eBay account** sets the fees in **What you keep**: private or business, and for a business, whether you are registered for VAT. Your eBay account settings say which yours is. It is only used to work out your profit.

## 4. Your account

Under **Account**:

- **Name**: what we call you.
- **Password**: change it, or set one. Every other device is signed out.
- **Passkeys**: choose **Add a passkey on this device** to sign in with Face ID, a fingerprint or your screen lock.
- **Emails from Joseph**: turn the short tips emails on or off. Receipts, codes and your listings always come.

To change your email, write to us from the one you use now.

## Deleting your account

Choose **Delete account**, then **Ask us to delete it**. This opens an email to us; we delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email. Unused credits bought in the last 14 days are refunded.

## 1. Open the sign-up page

Go to [my.remvo.app](https://my.remvo.app) and choose **Open an account**, or tap **Make your first listing** anywhere on this site.

## 2. Enter your email and a password

Type your email, then a password in **Choose a password**. It needs at least 10 characters; a short sentence works well, and the hint under the box counts down until it says **Long enough**.

Choose **Open my account**.

## 3. Type the code from your email

We email you a six-digit code. Type or paste it into **Six-digit code** and it goes in by itself. You can also open the link in the email on the same device.

Not there after a minute? Check spam or promotions. It comes from Remvo. Choose **Send again** for a new one, and use the newest email.

## 4. Make your first listing

You land on Home, signed in. Your account comes with 3 welcome credits, enough for one listing, so your first one costs nothing. Welcome credits last 30 days.

## Good to know

- Remvo never asks for your eBay login. You copy the listing into eBay yourself.
- You can add a passkey later in **Profile**, to sign in with Face ID, a fingerprint or your screen lock.

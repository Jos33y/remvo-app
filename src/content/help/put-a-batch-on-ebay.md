## 1. Download the eBay file

Open the batch from **Listings** (batches from the last 7 days are at the top). Under **Put them on eBay**, choose **Download for eBay.com**, or your own eBay site.

You can download it while the batch is still running; it has the listings ready so far.

## 2. Upload it to eBay

In eBay **Seller Hub**, open **Reports**, then **Uploads**, and upload the file. Each listing becomes a draft on eBay, usually within 15 minutes.

## 3. Finish and publish the drafts

In your eBay drafts, add your photos and copy the item specifics across from Remvo, then publish. eBay keeps a draft for 75 days after its last edit.

For every step on the eBay side, see [how to publish your eBay drafts in bulk](/guides/how-to-publish-ebay-drafts-in-bulk).

## Everything in one file

**Download everything as CSV** has every field, the item specifics as columns, and why any line did not work. Use **To check** on the batch page to see lines where a barcode matched more than one product, or where eBay asks for something to fill in.

## 1. Open the listing

Go to **Listings** and open it. At the top: the photo, the title, the price from live eBay listings, and how many item specifics are filled.

## 2. Start your listing on eBay

On eBay, choose to create a listing. Then work down **Copy into eBay** in Remvo, which is in the order eBay asks: **Title**, **Condition**, **Description**, **Category**, **Brand**, **MPN** and the **UPC** or **EAN**, the **Item specifics**, the **Parcel** and the **Price**.

## 3. Copy each field

Tap the copy button beside a field and paste it into eBay. The **Category** button copies the category ID, for eBay's category box. The **Item specifics** button copies them all at once, and each one can also be copied on its own.

To change something first, choose **Edit**, then **Save**. **Put back** undoes it.

## 4. Fill in what is missing

**Still needed on eBay** lists what eBay asks for that the product page does not say, such as the colour of the one in your hand. Fill those in from the item.

Check it against your stock, add your photos, then publish on eBay.

## Also on the listing

- **What you keep**: your profit after eBay's fees. Choose **Change** to set private or business seller.
- **Copy everything** copies every field at once, and **Download CSV** saves the listing as a file.
- Listings are kept for 90 days, then deleted.

## 1. Open Bulk listing

On **Home**, choose **Got a stack?**, or in **Listings** choose **Bulk listing** (**Bulk** on a phone).

## 2. Add your products, one a line

Any of these work, up to 500 at a time:

- **Paste** a column of barcodes or product links from a spreadsheet.
- **Upload a CSV**. A column named Condition is used for each line, and a column named Cost gives each listing what you keep after eBay's fees. From Excel or Google Sheets, save or download the sheet as CSV first.
- **Scan barcodes** with your phone's camera.
- **A barcode scanner**: click in the sheet and scan; each one lands on its own line.

## 3. Choose the condition

**Condition** is used for every line that does not have its own.

## 4. Check, free

Choose **Check, free**. Remvo reads every line and tells you how many are ready to make, which repeats it left out, and which lines need a look. Tap one of those to jump to it and fix it. Nothing is spent.

## 5. Start the batch

The cost is 3 credits a listing. Choose **Start**, and the batch page shows each listing as it is made, about 20 seconds each.

You can leave the screen; we email you when the batch is done. A line that does not work gives its credits back. Next: [put the batch on eBay](/help/put-a-batch-on-ebay).

## If it does not work

- "That is a spreadsheet file": save it as CSV, then upload that.
- Short of credits? Top up, or choose **Start the first** to make as many as your balance covers.

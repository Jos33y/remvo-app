## 1. Choose your eBay site

Open the [eBay fee calculator](/ebay-fee-calculator) and choose **eBay.com** or **eBay.co.uk**. On eBay.co.uk, choose **Private seller** or **Business seller**; for a business, tick **Registered for VAT** if you are, and pick the category.

## 2. Type what it sold for

Enter the price in **Sold for**, and what the buyer pays for postage. The top line shows what eBay takes. Tap **eBay fees** to see each fee.

## 3. Add your costs

Add **Item cost**, the postage you pay, any **Promoted Listings** rate and **Other costs** such as packaging. The top line now shows what you keep, your margin and the price you break even at.

## 4. Work back from a profit

Type **Profit you want**, and the calculator says what to sell it for to keep it.

## Good to know

- The rates come from eBay's own fee pages, and the page says when they were last checked.
- It is free, with no account.
- Every Remvo listing shows the same sum, in **What you keep**.

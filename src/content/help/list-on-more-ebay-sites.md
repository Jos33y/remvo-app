## 1. Enter the product

On **Home**, type or scan the barcode, or paste the link, and choose the condition. Instead of the main button, choose **Or list it on more eBay sites**.

## 2. Choose the sites

Under **Which eBay sites?**, tick the ones you sell on, or choose **All seven**: eBay.com, eBay.ca, eBay.co.uk, eBay.de, eBay.fr, eBay.it and eBay.es. Your own site is ticked first.

Each site gets its own listing, written in its language, in its categories, with its parcel service and a price from its own live listings.

## 3. Make them

The button says the cost:

| Sites | Credits |
|---|---|
| 1 | 3 |
| 2 | 6 |
| 3 | 8 |
| 4 | 11 |
| 5 | 14 |
| 6 or 7 | 15 |

A site that does not work gives its credits back.

## 4. See where it brings in most

As soon as two sites have a price, **Where it brings in most** ranks them by what you get after VAT and eBay's fees, in your currency. Open any site's listing to copy it into that eBay site.

**Download every site as CSV** gives one row a site, with every field.

## 1. Sign in with your password

Go to [my.remvo.app](https://my.remvo.app). Enter your **Email** and **Password**, then choose **Sign in**.

## 2. Or sign in with a passkey

If you have added a passkey, choose **Sign in with a passkey** and use Face ID, a fingerprint or your screen lock. On many browsers your passkey is also offered as soon as you tap the email box.

To add one, go to **Profile**, then **Add a passkey on this device**.

## 3. Forgot your password?

Choose **Forgot password?** beside the password box. Enter your email and choose **Email me a code**.

Type the six-digit code from the email, choose a **New password**, then **Save and sign in**. Every other device you were signed in on is signed out.

## If it does not work

- "That passkey did not work": try again, or sign in with your password.
- No email? Check spam or promotions, then choose **Send again**. Use the newest code.

## 1. Open Credits

Choose **Credits** in the app's menu. At the top: how many listings you have left, and until when your credits last.

## 2. Choose a pack

Pick your currency, then a pack:

| Pack | Credits | Listings |
|---|---|---|
| $5 (£4) | 50 | 16 |
| $20 (£16) | 200 | 66 |
| $50 (£40) | 550, with 50 bonus | 183 |
| $100 (£80) | 1,150, with 150 bonus | 383 |

Or enter **Your own amount**, from $5 to $1,000, at $0.10 a credit.

## 3. Pay through Polar

Checkout opens on Polar, our merchant of record. You see the total, with any tax, before you pay. Remvo never sees your card details.

## 4. Your credits arrive

Back in Remvo, the page says the payment is received and adds the credits, usually within seconds. If Polar takes longer, you can leave the page; the credits appear on their own.

## Good to know

- No subscription. You pay only when you top up.
- Credits last 12 months, and every top-up extends all the credits you have bought.
- A listing that does not finish gives its credits back on its own.
- Unused credits are refunded in full if you ask within 14 days. See the [credit terms](/credits).
- Paid and the credits have not arrived after a few minutes? [Talk to a person](/help/talk-to-a-person).

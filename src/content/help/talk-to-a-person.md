## 1. Open Support

In the app, choose **Support** in the menu. A number on it means you have a reply.

## 2. Write your message

Start with one of the buttons, such as **A listing went wrong** or **Credits or a payment**, or just write. A listing's ref, or a batch's, helps us find it fast.

A person answers, usually within a few hours. If you have left the app, the reply also goes to your email.

## 3. From a listing that did not work

On a failed listing, choose **Want a person to look at it?**. The message starts itself with the listing's ref.

## Not signed in?

Use the chat on any page of this site: tap **Chat with us** and leave your email for the reply. Or write to support@remvo.app; every email is answered within 48 hours.

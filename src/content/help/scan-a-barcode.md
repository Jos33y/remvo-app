## 1. Open the camera

On your phone, on **Home**, tap the camera button at the end of the **Make a listing** box. The first time, your phone asks if Remvo can use the camera: choose Allow.

The button shows on phones and tablets. On a computer, type the barcode or use a barcode scanner.

## 2. Point at the barcode

Hold the barcode on the box inside the frame, a hand's width away. When it is read, your phone buzzes, the camera closes and the numbers are in the box.

Nothing is spent yet. Choose the condition, then **Make listing, 3 credits**.

## 3. Scanning many boxes

In **Bulk listing**, choose **Scan barcodes**. Each barcode you scan goes onto its own line, and the screen shows **Added** with its number. A barcode already on the sheet is not added twice.

When you have them all, choose **Done**. You can scan up to 500 at a time. In a dark room, use **Light on**, if your phone offers it.

## If it does not work

- "The camera is blocked for Remvo": allow the camera for my.remvo.app in your browser's settings, or type the barcode instead.
- It will not read? Move a little further away, hold it still, and make sure the whole barcode is in the frame. Or choose **Type it instead**.

The camera's picture is read on your phone and never sent to us or anyone else; only the numbers go into the box.

## 1. Enter the barcode or link

On **Home**, type the barcode into **Make a listing**: the 12 or 13 numbers under the stripes on the box. An Amazon product link works too.

On a phone, you can tap the camera button at the end of the box to [scan the barcode](/help/scan-a-barcode) instead.

## 2. Choose the condition

Under **Condition**, pick one:

| Condition | When |
|---|---|
| New | Sealed, never used |
| New other | Open box, unused |
| Used | Has been used |
| For parts | Not working, or sold for spares |

## 3. Make it

Choose **Make listing, 3 credits**. If one barcode matches more than one product, Remvo asks **Which product?** first. Choose yours; nothing is spent until you do.

You see each step as it happens: reading the product page, finding the category, filling in the item specifics, measuring the parcel, checking prices and writing the title.

## 4. Your listing is ready

In about 20 seconds the listing opens, with every field in the order eBay asks for it. Next, [copy it into eBay](/help/copy-a-listing-into-ebay).

You can leave the screen while it is being made. It carries on and appears in **Listings**.

## If it does not work

- "That barcode does not check out": a digit is missing or extra. Barcodes are 8, 12, 13 or 14 digits.
- If a listing does not finish, its 3 credits come straight back to your balance.
- The hint under the box says which eBay site the listing is for. Choose **Change** to pick another.
